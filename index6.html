KOSMI

Virtual Rooms • Video • Chat • Games
Kosmi is an online virtual room platform that allows people to connect and spend time together over the internet. Users can create a virtual room and invite other people to join using a link. Inside the room, users can communicate through video and voice calls, text chat, and screen sharing. They can also watch videos together, play games, and share content with other people in the room. Kosmi is designed to provide a shared online space where friends, family, classmates, or groups can interact and enjoy activities together. It works mainly through a web browser, so users can join rooms without needing to install a traditional desktop application. In simple terms, Kosmi combines video calling, chatting, screen sharing, entertainment, and online activities into one shared virtual room.

INTERNET ARCHIVE

Books • Movies • Music • Websites • Software
Internet Archive is a free digital library that provides access to millions of books, movies, music, websites, software, images, and other digital materials. It is a non-profit organization focused on preserving digital content and making it available to people around the world. Users can search for and view or download many items, depending on their availability and copyright restrictions. Internet Archive also operates the Wayback Machine, which allows users to view archived versions of websites from the past. It is widely used for research, education, historical preservation, and accessing older digital content.
